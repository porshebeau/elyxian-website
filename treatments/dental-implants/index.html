Dental implants in Lajpat Nagar

A titanium post placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth, a bridge or a denture. Implants take months rather than days, so we explain each stage before you start.

Who it suits

Is it right for you?

Implants suit adults with one or more missing teeth who have healthy gums and enough bone in the jaw to hold them. If there isn’t enough bone, a bone graft may make an implant possible.

Some things affect how well an implant heals: smoking, diabetes that isn’t well controlled, gum disease, and some medicines, including certain treatments for osteoporosis. Tell us about these and we will discuss what they mean for you.

Implants are not the only way to replace a tooth. A bridge or a denture may suit you better, and we explain the differences, including cost and how long each tends to last.

An implant can support

  • A single crown, replacing one tooth
  • A bridge across several missing teeth
  • A denture that clips in place so it doesn’t move

What to expect

What happens, step by step

  1. 1

    Assessment

    An examination and imaging, usually X-rays and sometimes a 3D scan, to check the bone and plan where the implant goes. We also go through your medical history.

  2. 2

    Placing the implant

    The implant is placed in the jaw under local anaesthesia. Most people find the next few days similar to having a tooth out.

  3. 3

    Healing

    The bone grows around the implant, which commonly takes about three to six months. A temporary tooth can often be worn in the meantime.

  4. 4

    The final tooth

    Once the implant is secure, we take an impression or scan and fit the crown, bridge or denture made for it.

Aftercare

Looking after an implant

For the first few days, eat soft food, avoid chewing on that side, and follow the cleaning advice we give you. Some swelling and discomfort is normal and usually eases within a week.

Don’t smoke. It slows healing and raises the risk of the implant failing.

In the long run, an implant needs the same care as a natural tooth: brushing twice a day, cleaning around it daily, and regular reviews. Gum disease around an implant (peri-implantitis) can cause it to fail.

Questions

What people ask about dental implants

Does getting an implant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so you should feel pressure but not pain. Afterwards, most people manage the soreness with ordinary pain relief for a few days.

How long do dental implants last?

With good cleaning and regular reviews, implants can last for many years. The crown on top may need replacing at some point because of wear. Smoking and gum disease shorten an implant’s life.

How long does the whole process take?

Usually several months from assessment to the final tooth, mainly because the bone needs time to heal around the implant. It takes longer if you need a bone graft first.

Am I too old for an implant?

Age alone is rarely the deciding factor. What matters more is your general health, the bone in your jaw, and whether you can keep the area clean.
