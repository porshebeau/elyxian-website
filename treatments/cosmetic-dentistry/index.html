Who it suits

Is it right for you?

Cosmetic treatment suits adults who would like to change how their teeth look: their colour, a chip, a gap or uneven edges. Your teeth and gums need to be healthy first, so any decay or gum problems are treated before cosmetic work begins.

Some changes can be undone and some cannot. Whitening and bonding remove little or no tooth surface. Veneers usually need a thin layer of enamel removed, and that is permanent. We explain this before you decide.

Treatments we can discuss

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Composite bonding to repair chips or close small gaps
  • Veneers to change the shape or shade of front teeth
  • Replacing old, discoloured fillings with tooth-coloured ones

Aftercare

Keeping the result

Whitening can make teeth sensitive for a day or two, and its effect fades with time. Tea, coffee, turmeric and tobacco stain teeth faster.

Bonding and veneers can chip, as natural teeth can. Avoid biting very hard objects, and wear a night guard if you grind your teeth.

Clean them as you would your natural teeth, and come in for regular reviews.

Questions

What people ask about cosmetic dentistry

Is teeth whitening safe?

When it is planned and supervised by a dentist, whitening is generally safe. The most common side effect is temporary sensitivity. Lemon juice, baking soda and charcoal can wear away enamel, and that damage is permanent.

How long does whitening last?

It varies from person to person. The effect fades gradually, and faster with tea, coffee, tobacco and strongly coloured foods. Top-up treatment can be discussed.

Will whitening change the colour of my crowns or fillings?

No. Whitening only works on natural tooth. If you have crowns or fillings at the front, we plan around them, and they are sometimes replaced afterwards to match.

What is the difference between bonding and veneers?

Bonding is tooth-coloured filling material shaped directly on the tooth, usually in one visit and with little or no drilling. Veneers are thin shells, usually porcelain, made to fit and bonded to the front of the tooth. They resist staining and tend to last longer, but usually need some enamel removed.
