Emergency instructions

        
If it can’t wait

        
Call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department if you have swelling that is spreading to your eye or neck, difficulty breathing or swallowing, bleeding that won’t stop, or a serious injury to the face or head.

        

          Call the clinic
          WhatsApp the clinic
        

        

          
Knocked-out adult tooth
Hold it by the crown and rinse it briefly without scrubbing. Try to place it back in the socket, or keep it in milk. See a dentist straight away, ideally within the hour.

          
Severe toothache
Rinse with warm salt water and take over-the-counter pain relief as directed on the pack. Do not place aspirin on the gum. Contact us as soon as you can.

          
Broken tooth or lost filling
Rinse your mouth, keep any pieces, and avoid chewing on that side. A cold compress on your cheek can reduce swelling. Contact us to arrange a visit.

        

        
Outside clinic hours, send a WhatsApp message and the clinic will reply at the earliest opening.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        
Privacy notice

        
What happens to your information

        
        
Elyxian Dental & Smile Studio is responsible for the information you share with us through this website. Here it is in plain language.

      

      


        
What we collect

        
Your name, phone number, preferred date and time, the treatment you are interested in, and any message you choose to add.


        
Why we collect it

        
Only to contact you about your appointment. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell it.


        
How it reaches us

        
This website has no database and does not store what you type in the form. When you continue, your details are written into a WhatsApp message that you check and send yourself. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to that message. If you call or email instead, your details reach us that way. Only clinic staff handle your request.


        
How long we keep it

        
We keep booking messages for up to 12 months and then delete them, unless you become a patient. Patient records are kept as long as the law and professional rules require.


        
Your rights

        
You can withdraw your consent at any time, and ask to see, correct or delete your details. If you are booking for someone under 18, a parent or guardian gives consent on their behalf. To do any of this, write to the grievance officer below.


        
Grievance officer

        
Name of grievance officer, Elyxian Dental & Smile Studio, Building and street, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi 110024. Email: grievance@example.com.


        
Analytics, cookies and third parties

        
This website uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser’s IP address reaches Google. The map loads from Google only if you choose to view it, and the links to WhatsApp and Google Maps take you to those services.
